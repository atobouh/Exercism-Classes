Everything in this chapter, on real-looking gear. You plug in a console cable to reach the router before it has an address, give its LAN port an address, cable the office with straight-through cables, and prove it works with a ping. Watch the switch's port lights while you work: they show spanning tree deciding whether each port is safe to use.
